# Why ValueApp exists

## The problem in practice

Planning effort in early phases is often discussed on a weak foundation:

- Construction costs are still uncertain, yet effort is somehow derived from them.
- Reference projects exist, but are rarely well documented or directly comparable.
- Adjustment factors are described intuitively rather than structurally (and later everyone wonders about deviations).
- Communication between clients, planning teams, and internal stakeholders often remains unclear.

The typical consequences are incorrect assumptions, friction, poor expectation management, and unnecessary iteration loops.

## What ValueApp does differently

ValueApp combines the ETH Value Method with a clear, guided application. It is based on:

1. **Measurable determinants**  
   Instead of “costs”, measurable quantities such as gross floor area (m²) are central.

2. **Reference-based baseline models**  
   Effort ranges are derived from realized projects — deliberately using ranges instead of false precision.

3. **Structured description of complexity**  
   Complexity is described along clear dimensions (design, technical, spatial, organizational) and systematized using the Leadership Framework.

4. **Transparent documentation**  
   Inputs, assumptions, and results are presented in a way that is traceable, communicable, and verifiable.

## Typical use cases

- **Early project phases:** estimating effort ranges for decision-making and proposal processes  
- **Plausibility checks:** comparing projects, variants, or usage mixes  
- **Communication:** creating a shared language for requirements and expectations  
- **Internal steering:** rough capacity and phase planning (depending on the organization)

## Distinction from cost or fee tools

ValueApp is neither a cost tool nor a fee tool.

- **Cost/fee tools:** typically “cost → fee”, strongly dependent on benchmarks and level of detail  
- **ValueApp:** “determinant + reference data + adjustment factors → effort (h)”, focusing on transparency and plausibility  

This means: ValueApp can support fee discussions, but does not replace service definitions, contractual logic, or project-specific clarifications.

## Why ranges matter

In early phases, “a single number” is almost always misleading.  
Ranges make uncertainty visible instead of hiding it.

The app therefore aims for:
- traceable ranges  
- comparable indicators  
- documented assumptions
