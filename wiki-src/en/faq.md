## General

### Does the ValueApp replace fee regulations such as the SIA LHO?

No. The ValueApp implements the **ETH Value Method** and provides transparent, traceable effort ranges based on determinants and complexity.  
It can be used together with fee regulations such as the SIA LHO to derive fair and non-discriminatory fee proposals.

### Why does the app not use construction costs as an input?

Construction costs depend heavily on market conditions, timing, and procurement strategy and can introduce distortions.  
The Value Method instead uses determinants such as **gross floor area**, which are better documented and comparable across projects.

### Is the result a fixed number of hours or a range?

The base models provide **prediction intervals**, not a seemingly precise single value.  
Adjustment factors and inputs result in a **plausible range**, which can be translated into planning assumptions and fee models.

## Using the app

### How detailed should the project description be?

A short paragraph describing the context, task, and key conditions is sufficient.  
The description should be clear enough that someone else can understand the project and interpret the hours even a year later.

### How should I handle mixed-use projects (e.g. residential and retail)?

Enter the total gross floor area and distribute percentages across uses.  
The app combines the corresponding base models. Very small shares can optionally be ignored.

### What if I am unsure about the complexity?

Start with your best estimate and briefly document your reasoning.  
Changes in the Leadership Framework directly show how the effort is affected.  
**Transparency is more important than a “perfect” value.**

### Can I override the hours per phase?

Yes. The suggested distribution is based on typical projects.  
For project-specific deviations, you can adjust the percentages.  
A short justification improves traceability.

## Technology and data

### How often are the base models updated?

The base models are periodically reviewed and refined using newly realized projects to reflect current practice.

### Can the ValueApp be integrated into existing IT systems?

Yes. The core logic can be embedded as a widget into external platforms and portals.  
Technical documentation and examples are available in the  
→ [Integration repository](https://github.com/a-r-c-experts/core-value-integration).

### Where can I report errors or suggest improvements?

Use the **feedback button** in the app.  
There you can describe issues and, if needed, refer to a specific project or screenshot.
